Features

Trigger Modes

Every binding pairs which button with how it fires. Control Flex offers ten trigger modes, so one button can tap, hold, latch, and double-press for different actions.

The Ten Modes

Mode When it fires Typical use
PressOnce, on button downOne-shots and menu opens
ReleaseOnce, on button upLayer return, “on let go” actions
HoldActive the whole time heldMovement, look, sustained use, layer switches
TapOnce, on release after a short press; if held past the threshold, nothing fires on releaseQuick actions that must not fire on a hold
Long PressOnce, after being held past the long-press thresholdA second function on the same button (e.g. pause menu)
ToggleFirst press activates and holds, second press releases; independent of button hold (RELEASE events are ignored)Persistent states: sprint, walk mode, POV
Double PressA second press inside the tap window fires once immediately on down (not latched)An extra one-shot alongside Hold / Press
Double TapTwo short presses; fires once on the second releaseConfirm-style actions that must not steal the first tap
Double HoldShort press, then press-and-hold inside the tap window; stays active until releaseTap-then-hold for deliberate triggers that resist misfires
Double ToggleSame latch as Toggle; flips on the second short-press releaseA latch sharing one button with its Hold variant (Basic does this for sneak)

Thresholds & Time Windows

  • Tap and Long Press thresholds are global, 100–2000 ms, configured in Other Settings. They apply to every binding and template.
  • The Tap threshold doubles as the gap limit for every Double mode: Double Press / Tap / Hold / Toggle all use it as the maximum interval between the two presses.
  • Defaults: Tap 500 ms, Long Press 1000 ms (from the Basic template).

Changing a Mode

  • In Key Mapping, the Mode column cycles through the common modes (Press / Hold / Tap / Long Press).
  • Templates and compat JSON support all ten, written as button|MODE — for example buttonA|HOLD or rightStickClick|DOUBLE_TOGGLE. Mode tokens: PRESS / RELEASE / HOLD / TAP / LONG_PRESS / TOGGLE / DOUBLE_PRESS / DOUBLE_TAP / DOUBLE_HOLD / DOUBLE_TOGGLE.
  • Some actions restrict their mode: layer switches are fixed to HOLD, Shift-layer return-to-Main to RELEASE, and radial-menu opens support PRESS and HOLD.

Worked Examples

Button Binding Effect
R-stick click HOLD + DOUBLE_TOGGLE Hold to sneak, double-tap to latch sneak (Basic default)
Start LONG_PRESS Long press opens the pause menu; short presses do nothing
D-Pad → TAP Tap to change perspective; an accidental hold does nothing

To combine buttons into one action, see Combo Keys; to bake toggles into a template, see Configuration.
