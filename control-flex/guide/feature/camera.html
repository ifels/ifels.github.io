功能详解Features

相机与瞄准Camera & Aim

右摇杆控制视角，按渲染帧驱动偏航 / 俯仰以保证平滑；三种模式覆盖从单机到战斗模组的不同需求。 The right stick drives the camera, updating yaw and pitch at render frame rate for smoothness. Three modes cover everything from vanilla play to combat mods.

三种视角模式Three Look Modes

模式Mode 说明What it does 适合Best for
Direct Turn 摇杆直接改变视角，响应最快Stick moves the camera directly; the most responsive 快速转身、后台窗口（分屏）Fast turns and background windows (split-screen)
Smooth Turn 带平滑过渡的转向，观感更接近主机游戏Turns with smoothing, closer to a console feel 日常游玩、需要“跟随感”的场合Everyday play where a follow-through feel is wanted
Simulate Mouse 视角经鼠标管线（MouseHandler）输出 —— 让拦截鼠标的模组也能收到准星移动Look is routed through the mouse pipeline (MouseHandler), so mods that intercept the mouse still see crosshair movement Epic Fight 等自定义瞄准的战斗模组（新安装默认开启）Combat mods with custom aiming such as Epic Fight (on by default for new installs)

灵敏度与响应Sensitivity & Response

  • 灵敏度：1–100 档；另有 1.0–10.0 的倍数项（默认 1.0）。Sensitivity: a 1–100 slider plus a 1.0–10.0 multiplier (default 1.0).
  • 响应曲线：二次曲线，让小幅推杆更精细；在相机设置里调节，曲线数据与摇杆死区同在校准体系内。Response curve: a quadratic curve that gives finer control at small deflections, tuned in Camera Settings; it belongs to the same Calibration family as stick deadzones.
  • Y 轴反转：可选项，在相机设置里切换。Y-axis invert: optional, toggled in Camera Settings.
  • 帧率无关：视角更新使用帧增量，高刷新率下不会变快、低帧率下不会变迟钝。Frame-rate independent: look uses the frame delta, so it neither speeds up at high refresh rates nor lags at low ones.

望远镜（Zoom）Zoom

望远镜降速（缩小视角时的灵敏度补偿）仅在模拟鼠标模式下生效；这与原版望远镜的行为保持一致，也是其他模式需要的取舍。望远镜动作本身是一个可绑定项（Basic 模板默认未绑）。 Zoom slowdown (sensitivity compensation while scoping) only applies in Simulate Mouse mode, matching what mods expect from the mouse pipeline. Zoom itself is a bindable action (unbound in the Basic template).

分屏场景的注意点Split-Screen Caveat

Simulate Mouse 无法驱动后台窗口的系统光标，因此在失焦窗口里会自动回退为 Direct Turn。同机双人建议直接使用 Direct Turn 或 Smooth Turn（见分屏双人）。 Simulate Mouse cannot drive the OS cursor in a background window, so it automatically falls back to Direct Turn there. For couch co-op, use Direct Turn or Smooth Turn (see Split-Screen Co-op).
